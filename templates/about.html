1. Why We Built this Digital Backup

Classic Art Archive was founded to solve a fundamental problem of modern digital curation. While millions enjoy our daily explorations on Instagram (@classicartarchive) and Facebook, social media platforms inevitably downscale images, enforce restrictive aspect ratios, and subject educational content to erratic algorithm shifts.

This web portal serves as our independent permanent vault: every featured painting is documented with its original historical context and presented in uncompressed Ultra HD museum resolution — sourced directly from world-class open-access museum collections.
